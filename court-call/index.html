Let's be clear

It's players, not an institution.

Court Call is a community run by players, for players. Here's what it isn't.

A club
Just players

No board, no membership tiers, no committee. Peer to peer.

A paid service
Free to join

No fees, no upsell. It costs nothing to find a game.

Run by a venue
Independent

Not tied to any court or facility. Play wherever you like.

Self-hosted. Your data stays yours.

Court Call runs on our own infrastructure — not a third-party platform monetizing your activity. No data selling, no ad tracking. You're here to find a game, not to be the product.
